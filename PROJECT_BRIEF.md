# Kalava — Project Brief

Confirmed through discovery on 7 October 2026.

## Objective

Build and launch a real, working ecommerce website for Kalava. The initial target is launch on 7 October 2026, subject to receiving the assets, business details and account access needed for launch. This brief records the agreed direction; building has not been authorized yet.

## Products and Customers

- Sell framed art prints in the wall décor category.
- Launch with 6–15 products priced at approximately ₹3,000–₹5,000 each.
- Serve young people decorating their first home and families refreshing their homes.
- Appeal to modern and traditional tastes through a warm, approachable presentation.

## Artwork and Production

- Kalava's graphic designers will create the artwork in 2D.
- Product listings will show the artwork in styled interior mockups.
- Finished physical products are not currently held as ready stock; framed prints will be produced after orders arrive.
- The owner prefers not to foreground the internal production process in customer-facing copy.
- Customer-facing listings must accurately describe the physical product, materials, dimensions and expected dispatch and delivery times. Do not imply ready stock or immediate dispatch unless accurate.
- Label interior mockups as visualisations and keep their scale, frame appearance and artwork faithful to the delivered product.
- This clarified asset plan supersedes the initial statement that existing product photographs needed editing. Final artwork and any available images still need to be supplied and prepared.

## Brand and Visual Direction

- Store name: Kalava.
- Personality: warm, earthy and minimal.
- Palette: cream, terracotta and charcoal (updated by the owner after the initial build).
- Typography direction: elegant, book-style headings with easy-to-read body text. Exact fonts remain to be selected.
- No reference websites were supplied.

## Payments

- Payment provider selection, account setup and activation remain outstanding.

## Customer Contact

- Contact form and business email.
- Business email address and destination for form submissions remain to be provided.

## Required Features

- Clear product dimensions and materials.
- A wall-size guide to help customers choose suitable sizes.
- Customer reviews.
- Customer photographs showing products in real homes when genuine content becomes available.
- Do not fabricate reviews or present styled mockups as customer photographs.

## Domain

- Owner reports already owning kalava.in.
- Domain access and connection to hosting remain to be arranged.

## Details Needed Before Launch

- Final product names, artwork files, descriptions and individual prices.
- Print sizes, frame options, materials and any variants.
- Accurate production, dispatch and delivery timelines.
- Shipping coverage, charges and fulfilment arrangements.
- Returns, cancellation and damage-resolution rules appropriate to the products.
- Business identity, support email and applicable billing details.
- Payment account setup and domain access.
- Genuine review and customer-photo content, if available.

These are outstanding implementation inputs, not assumed decisions. The ₹3,000–₹5,000 range should guide positioning; an affordable-price promise has not been established.
