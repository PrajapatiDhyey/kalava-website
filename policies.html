Shipping & delivery

Shipping areas, charges and dispatch timelines are being finalised. Please request confirmation for your address before placing an order or paying. No free shipping or delivery date is promised by this preview.

Returns & cancellations

The final returns, cancellations and damage-resolution policy will be published before the store accepts payments. Ask Kalava to confirm the applicable terms before you buy.

Your information

Your shopping bag is stored in your browser using localStorage. This site does not save your contact or delivery information to a server. Checkout prepares a WhatsApp message containing the details you enter; you choose whether to send it. The contact form prepares an email in your email app.

WhatsApp, your email provider and payment apps handle information under their own policies. Google Fonts is used to display the site’s fonts and may receive your IP address when fonts load. No analytics or advertising trackers have been added.
